# Retailer access posture: public endpoints only, hard backoff, no protection bypass

Maqrivo integrates retailers exclusively through public, no-authentication endpoints and open data. The first slice ships Carrefour store discovery (verified public eligibility API), Carrefour catalogue/product endpoints behind a default-on config flag (low frequency, per enabled store, identifying User-Agent, hard backoff at the first 403/429, kill-switch env), Intermarché prospectus ingestion from its public flipbook host, and Open Prices as the price backbone. Blocked runs degrade to manual entry with evidence — they are never escalated: no browser emulation against bot management, no CAPTCHA solving, no header spoofing beyond an identifying User-Agent. Programmatic use of catalogue aggregators (Bonial et al.) is rejected outright: CGU bans, robots-blocked endpoints (verified 2026-09-04); users consult leaflets as humans and enter promotions manually.

This is a deliberate scope limit: French retailer CGU extraction bans (enforceable per CJEU Ryanair C-30/14) make "tolerated public endpoint + honest identity + tiny volume + graceful degradation" the only posture consistent with the no-evidence-no-deal principle and with sustainable personal-scale operation.
